## Playing the game is not "using the material"

Printing the deck for your family, your classroom, your clinic or your support group, and playing it with people, is ordinary use. So is translating it for people you work with, or making an Easy Read or AAC version for someone who needs one. None of that needs permission and none of it is commercial.

ShareAlike applies when you **distribute** an adaptation — publishing your translation, or a version you have re-set — not when you make one for the people in front of you.
