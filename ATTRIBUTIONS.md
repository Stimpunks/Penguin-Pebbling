It was first published at Autistic Realms: <https://autisticrealms.com/penguin-pebbling-a-game-of-creating-belonging-building-connection-and-understanding-autistic-identity/> — post ID 10380, published 2026-05-23, last modified 2026-05-24.

## The card artwork

The thirty-five card faces — thirty prompt cards and five locution cards — are Helen Edgar's, © 2026. Each card carries the credit line *"Autistic Realms & Stimpunks © 2026"* in the artwork itself.

The originals in `cards/print/` are exactly as downloaded from autisticrealms.com on 2026-09-14, renamed from Helen's upload numbering (`9-1.png`) to something legible (`infodumping-1.png`) and otherwise untouched. The WebP files in `cards/` are resized derivatives generated by `tools/make-images.py`; the PNGs are the masters.

## The prompts

The thirty card prompts are Helen's and Ryan's, parsed out of the original page rather than retyped, so they are accurate to the character. They live in `penguin-pebbling.js`.

## The PDFs

The Plain Language guide, the Easy Read guide, and the printable card deck in `downloads/` are Helen's, mirrored from autisticrealms.com. They are the free versions she publishes. **The shop link is hers and stays** — it is where the deck can be paid for or donated against, and it supports the work.

## What the game is grounded in

The **Five Neurodivergent Love Locutions** were coined by **Amythest Schaber** (Neurowonderful) and documented by **Stimpunks Foundation** (2022): <https://stimpunks.org/2022/01/22/the-five-neurodivergent-love-languages-2/>

The penguin pebbling locution draws on Helen Edgar's earlier writing at Autistic Realms (Edgar, 2023): <https://autisticrealms.com/penguin-pebbling-an-autistic-love-language/>

It is framed through:

- **Milton, D. (2012).** On the ontological status of autism: The 'double empathy problem'. *Disability & Society, 27*(6), 883–887.
- **Murray, D., Lesser, M., & Lawson, W. (2005).** Attention, monotropism and the diagnostic criteria for autism. *Autism, 9*(2), 139–156.
